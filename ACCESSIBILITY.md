# Accessibility

Xray Extender is a LuCI application intended to work within the OpenWrt LuCI interface and its supported themes and browsers.

The project aims to keep controls understandable and usable, but it has not undergone a formal accessibility audit. It does not currently claim conformance with WCAG or any other accessibility standard.

Some parts of the interface, especially dynamic status updates, server tables, and checkbox-based actions, may present usability challenges depending on the browser, theme, screen size, or assistive technology in use.

## Reporting an accessibility issue

Accessibility feedback and focused improvement suggestions are welcome through the repository's issue tracker. Please include:

- The page or control where the problem occurs.
- What you were trying to do and what happened.
- Browser and version, LuCI theme, and device or screen size where relevant.
- Assistive technology and version, if applicable.
- Clear reproduction steps. Screenshots or short recordings can help, but remove any private information first.

Please avoid including personal or sensitive information in reports.
