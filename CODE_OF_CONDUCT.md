# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Xray Extender to be a respectful, constructive, and welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

We value clear technical discussion, good-faith questions, thoughtful reviews, and collaboration focused on improving the project.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Criticizing ideas and implementations, not people.
- Explaining technical disagreements calmly and in good faith.
- Respecting other people's privacy. Do not publish credentials, private configuration, personal data, or private correspondence without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, discriminatory or hateful language, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit, hide, or remove contributions and comments that violate this Code of Conduct, and may restrict participation when necessary. Actions will be proportionate to the situation.

## Reporting

Please report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner or maintainers through GitHub rather than turning the incident into a public argument. Do not use public issue reports for sensitive personal information or security vulnerabilities.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.
